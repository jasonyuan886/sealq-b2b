PVS02 Handheld Vacuum Sealer — Technical Specifications

Cordless portable vacuum pump compatible with all embossed valve bags and external vacuum canisters. Ideal for home kitchens, delis, catering operations, food trucks, and outdoor activities.

Suction Power-60 kPa
Motor TypeBrushless DC
Power InputDC 5V/1A · 5W
Battery1200mAh Li-ion
Battery Life~50 seals/charge (medium bag)
Charge Time~2 hours (USB-C)
Auto Shutoff120 seconds
DimensionsΦ48 × 195 mm
Weight210 g
Noise Level<60 dB
Heat SealingNo (suction-only)
Detachable CupYes · Overflow protection

⚡ Duty Cycle & Continuous Use Ratings

80–100 Seals per full charge (medium bag, 23°C)
≤120s Continuous run before auto-shutoff (motor protection)
~30s rest Recommended cooldown between extended sessions
Important: The PVS02 is a portable suction-type vacuum sealer designed for short-burst operation (8–15 seconds per bag). It is not a chamber machine and is not rated for continuous 24/7 commercial kitchen duty cycles. For high-volume back-of-house operations (100+ seals per hour), we recommend pairing 2–3 units in rotation. For industrial chamber sealer inquiries, contact us for our commercial line.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
500 units for sealers (standard wholesale tier), 3,000 pieces for vacuum bags. Sample orders start from 5 units. For private label/OEM orders, MOQ is typically 1,000 units for custom packaging and 5,000 units for custom colors.
How long is the lead time?
Sample orders: 7 days. Standard wholesale (500–4,999 units): 15–25 days. Volume orders (5,000+): 20–30 days. OEM/ODM with customization: 25–40 days depending on scope. Lead times are ex-factory; add shipping time based on your chosen method.
Can I order just a sample first?
Yes! Sample orders are available starting from 5 units. Sample pricing is higher than wholesale but credited back on your first bulk order. We ship samples via DHL/FedEx with 3-5 day delivery.
Do you provide product certifications?
CE, RoHS, and FCC certificates are available for all orders. Additional certifications (EAC for Russia/CIS, PSE for Japan, etc.) can be arranged for volume orders with additional lead time and cost. NSF/ETL for commercial foodservice is currently under evaluation.
What payment methods do you accept?
Standard orders: T/T wire transfer (30% deposit, 70% balance before shipment). Orders over $20,000: L/C at sight. Samples: PayPal or Western Union. We accept USD, EUR, and GBP via WorldFirst multi-currency account.
Are the bags compatible with other handheld vacuum sealers?
Yes. Our embossed valve bags use a standard one-way air valve that works with most handheld vacuum pump sealers on the market, including FoodSaver handheld, Nesco, and other compatible brands. This makes them ideal for aftermarket/accessory sales.
What is the warranty policy?
All units come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. DOA (Dead on Arrival) rate is guaranteed under 1%; any DOA units are replaced free of charge in your next shipment.
Can you handle dropshipping or Amazon FBA prep?
We support FBA shipping directly to Amazon warehouses (FBA label application, polybag, carton labeling). Dropshipping to end customers is not available on wholesale terms — we are a factory, not a retailer.
What is the duty cycle? Can I use this continuously in a commercial kitchen?
The PVS02 is rated for 80–100 seals per full charge with a 120-second auto-shutoff for motor protection. Recommended cooldown between extended sessions is 30 seconds. For high-volume operations (100+ seals/hour), we recommend rotating 2-3 units. It is suitable for catering, food trucks, and retail packaging; for NSF-required back-of-house restaurant use, please check local health codes.
Do you offer Net-30 payment terms?
Net-30 terms are available for approved volume partners ordering 5,000+ units per order with a track record of successful orders. New customers start with T/T 30/70 terms and can apply for credit terms after 3 successful orders.
