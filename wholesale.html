PVS02 Handheld Vacuum Sealer — Technical Specifications

Cordless portable vacuum pump compatible with all embossed valve bags and external vacuum canisters. Ideal for home kitchens, delis, catering operations, food trucks, and outdoor activities.

Suction Power-60 kPa
Motor TypeBrushless DC
Power InputDC 5V/1A · 5W
Battery1200mAh Li-ion
Battery Life~50 seals/charge (medium bag)
Charge Time~2 hours (USB-C)
Auto Shutoff120 seconds
DimensionsΦ48 × 195 mm
Weight210 g
Noise Level<60 dB
Heat SealingNo (suction-only)
Detachable CupYes · Overflow protection

⚡ Duty Cycle & Continuous Use Ratings

80–100 Seals per full charge (medium bag, 23°C)
≤120s Continuous run before auto-shutoff (motor protection)
~30s rest Recommended cooldown between extended sessions
Important: The PVS02 is a portable suction-type vacuum sealer designed for short-burst operation (8–15 seconds per bag). It is not a chamber machine and is not rated for continuous 24/7 commercial kitchen duty cycles. For high-volume back-of-house operations (100+ seals per hour), we recommend pairing 2–3 units in rotation. For industrial chamber sealer inquiries, contact us for our commercial line.
